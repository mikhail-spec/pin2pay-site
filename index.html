FAQ

Questions, answered

How long does it take to go live?

Median time from sign-up to first live transaction is 11 hours. KYB verification runs in parallel with integration, so most teams ship in test mode within 30 minutes and switch to production the next morning.

What payment methods do you support?

Cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover, JCB, UnionPay), wallets (Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal, Alipay, WeChat Pay), bank transfers (SEPA, ACH, Faster Payments, PIX), buy-now-pay-later (Klarna, Afterpay, Affirm), and stablecoin rails (USDC on Ethereum, Solana, Base).

Is there a contract or minimum volume?

No on both. The Starter plan stays free for as long as you stay under $50K monthly. Growth has no minimum — you pay 0.4% per transaction, that's it.

How does fraud protection work?

Every transaction passes through a real-time ML model trained on $40B of historical traffic. You set the risk appetite (low / medium / high) and PIN2PAY auto-tunes thresholds. False positives are reimbursed within 7 days.

Can we self-host?

Not the core processing rails — those need to live in our PCI-scope environment. But everything else (dashboard, reporting, webhooks consumer) is open source under MIT, fork it freely.

What happens if PIN2PAY goes down?

Multi-region active-active. Last unscheduled outage was 47 seconds in November 2025. Status page at status.pin2pay.com, with public RCA on every incident.
